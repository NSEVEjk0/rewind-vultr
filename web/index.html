Before an import: bring today’s CSV export and the update you plan to import. Choose the intended fields and a numeric change limit. Rewind holds risky cells and unknown IDs out, then runs the exact approved update on copies and independently checks every resulting value.

After a mistake: bring a good version, a copy taken just after the mistake, and today’s file. Describe the bad import. Rewind’s agent investigates the copies and proposes individual cells to restore.

If a damaged cell differs between the after-incident version and today, Rewind keeps today’s value. You review the proposed replacements and approve that exact plan.

A fresh checker verifies each result against the originals. Downloads open after every data check and every temporary environment’s removal passes.

Use UTF-8 CSV with a unique ID column, or consistent SQLite snapshots for recovery. Files can contain up to 1,000 records, 2–16 fields and 256 KiB each. Approve up to 1,000 exact cells per operation. Without the after-incident copy, choose damaged cells manually.

Import previews use updates to existing records by ID. Recheck when your live data changes. Snapshots show observed differences, not a complete edit history. Vultr AI receives your recovery description, file structure and change counts; the preflight path makes no model calls.
