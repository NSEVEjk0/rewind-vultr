Restore only what went wrong.

Bring a good version, a copy taken just after the mistake, and today’s file. Describe the bad import. Rewind’s agent investigates the copies and proposes individual cells to restore.

If a damaged cell differs between the after-incident version and today, Rewind keeps today’s value. You review the proposed replacements and approve that exact plan.

The repair runs on disposable copies. A fresh checker verifies the result against the originals. Download opens only after every data check and every temporary environment’s removal passes.

Use UTF-8 CSV with a unique ID column, or consistent SQLite snapshots. Files can contain up to 1,000 records, 2–16 fields and 256 KiB each. A repair can restore up to 1,000 cells. Without the after-incident copy, choose the damaged cells manually.

Snapshots show observed differences, not a complete edit history. Vultr AI receives your description, file structure and change counts. It does not automatically receive the records in your files.
