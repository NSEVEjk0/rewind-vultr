How you use Rewind

Bring the last good version, a copy taken immediately after the mistake, and today’s file. Describe the import. Rewind investigates the copies, proposes individual cells to restore, and shows overlapping later edits that will stay.

You approve the exact repair. The recovery runs in an isolated environment on Vultr, and a separate checker verifies every approved and protected value. Download the recovered CSV or SQLite copy when verification and cleanup pass.

Have only two versions? Choose the damaged cells yourself. A third version is needed to identify observed later edits to those cells.

The walkthrough also shows an executed containment check: a program that keeps running is stopped at its deadline, and its temporary container is removed.
