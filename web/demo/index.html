How you use Rewind

Upload a good backup and today's database copy. Choose the damaged fields and records, review what will change, and download a checked recovery copy. Every value outside your selection keeps today's data.

The first version accepts small SQLite snapshots with one matching table. Selected cells are replaced with their backup values, including any later edits in those same cells. The recovery app runs on Vultr; this page shares its completed demonstration.

The final video segment shows the recorded result of a separate fixed-program containment test on Vultr. It made no model request. The test ran for 2.003 seconds before the sandbox stopped it and removed its container.
